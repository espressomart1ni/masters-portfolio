    Shipment Risk and T&A Dashboard

    Summer Internship Project

    Most merchandising teams still open 15 spreadsheets to understand order status. CTA's ERP shows live status but has no T&A calendars, no forward target dates and no plan-versus-actual view, so risk stayed invisible until a milestone was missed.

    So I built the missing layer: a single risk-scored, ranked view of which styles need attention before the shipment buffer erodes.

    • Auto-generates T&A calendars per style and per merchandiser.
    • Only the Actual Date is entered; status, delay days, risk score and category are formulas.
    • KPI cards, a Top 10 worklist and Green/Yellow/Red flags, readable in under a minute.

    Framework

    raw data, derived metrics, aggregated summary, visual layer, decision.

    1. T&A plansper style, per merchandiser
    2. Data consolidationStyle_Master and Generated_TA
    3. Risk scoring engineweighted delay plus buffer penalty, one row per style
    4. DashboardKPIs, Top 10, hotspots
    5. Merchandiser actionfollow-up and escalation

    The model is rule-based and explainable by design. Next step: predictive delay forecasting from historical data, with today's score as the auditable baseline. Built as a standalone prototype, designed to sit inside CTA's existing ERP.

    Measuring sustainable fashion consumption

    The Absence of a Standardized Framework for Measuring Sustainable Fashion Consumption: A Critical Review and Conceptual Proposal

    Consumption drives most of a garment's lifetime impact, yet it is the stage measurement handles worst. LCA and the Higg Index score products and factories but stop at the point of sale, and consumer studies lean on self-reported surveys that predict behaviour poorly (about 30% of "ethical" intenders follow through). Studies contradict each other because everyone measures something different.

    The proposed fix: ISCM, five dimensions

    • Environmental perception
    • Social and ethical perception
    • Cognitive alignment the novel piece: the gap between what people say and do
    • Economic feasibility
    • Behavioural engagement acquisition, usage, reuse, disposal

    The same instinct as the CTA dashboard: a structured, comparable system where none existed.

    Segmenting Apparel Customers

    Retailers collect data on every customer but act in bulk: same email, same discount. Premium customers get discounted needlessly; price-sensitive ones are ignored until they churn. Using purchase frequency per month and average purchase value (INR), K-Means (K = 3, scaled features, seed 123) lets natural groups emerge instead of assuming them.

    Low value

    From groups to strategy

    KNN (K = 5, 70/30 split) slots every new customer into a segment automatically, without re-running clustering. One defines the strategy, the other operationalises it.

    0.9666667

    accuracy on a small sample: proof of method, not a production benchmark.

    • Premium: no-discount personalisation, early access, exclusives.
    • Frequent: bundles and loyalty thresholds to nudge toward Premium.
    • Low value: seasonal promos and discount-led reactivation.
